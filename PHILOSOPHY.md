# Philosophy

Every company is not just a bureaucratic entity with a trademark, managers and slaves. In many cases people will say that companies are evil, managers do nothing and get all the money, you should not work because managers won't notice you, or they'll steal your work and pretend they are the ones who did everything. Hiring managers are another layer of management. Consulting companies do things worse. People aren't pure, you can't be pure in a world of evil people. This list goes on — but every problem can be solved.

These are the values Draqun is built on. Not rules for a company, but beliefs about how people should treat each other at work.

## For Hiring Managers

Don't be afraid. Know your team — talk with people daily, even if it's just 5-30 minutes per person. Ask them directly if they want someone new. Have candidates ready. Trust what you know about your team over stacks of documents and surveys. And don't steal other people's ideas to get promoted.

## For Employees

Don't be afraid. Share your thoughts on how the company could make more money — put it in writing officially. That way, if the company runs with your idea and the boss pockets the gains, there's a record. Make sure others know too.

## For Managers

You are the decision maker, not the idea generator — that's everyone's job. A company isn't a government machine. It's a group of people who want a house, groceries, a car, a family, their health. Treat it like that.

Think of other companies as other groups of people trying to survive, not enemies. Every company could potentially join yours — just make sure no one on their team loses anything in the deal; otherwise, don't make it.

Stealing clients hurts real people on the other side. Analytics matter, but don't make users angry.
